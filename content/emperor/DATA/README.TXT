EMPEROR: BATTLE FOR DUNE - Map Pack #3
==========================================

EMPEROR_MAP3.ZIP Contents:
	MAPS0003.RFD	8/28/01
	MAPS0003.RFH	8/28/01
	README.TXT	9/5/01

Maps Included:
+  "Carthag", 8-player, Geidi Prime.  A series of bridges cross the polluted oceans of the Baron's Homeworld, providing numerous strategic positions, and plenty of build-able area.
+  "Claw Rock", 3-player, Arrakis.  3 base positions connected to a common central rock area.  The spice areas are off to the left & right of each base point, and have infantry rock, trenches, etc. to divide spices fields in half.  Massive towering rocks shaped like claws frame the center.

Installation Instructions:

1. Unzip the contents of the zip file into your Emperor's Data directory.  (I.E. if you installed Emperor onto C:\WESTWOOD\EMPEROR\  then extract the files into C:\WESTWOOD\EMPEROR\DATA\   )
2. Run Emperor:Battle for Dune and look for the 2 new maps when you host a multiplayer game, or play a skirmish game.  They new maps appear in the map list along with the original multiplayer maps provided with the original CDs.
